# Top 1 Product Direction

## Product thesis

No queremos ser otra web fantasy que publica noticias, onces y opinion.
Queremos ser el copiloto que convierte informacion dispersa en decisiones claras para la liga real del usuario.

La frase de producto es:

`No te contamos LaLiga. Te decimos que hacer en tu fantasy.`

## What makes this different

1. Decision-first UX
   The first screen should answer:
   - who starts
   - who sits
   - who gets the captaincy
   - what move matters next

2. Confidence layer
   Every recommendation needs confidence, risk, and source freshness.

3. Market intelligence
   The product should not suggest generic hype names.
   It should suggest moves that improve the actual squad the user owns.

4. League-aware strategy
   The ideal next version changes advice depending on:
   - leader vs chaser mode
   - squad depth
   - budget
   - rival behavior

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Decision Copilot
- Action cards for today
- Confidence score for the lineup
- Matchday radar
- Key-player decisions
- Transfer recommendation cards

### Phase 2: Real League Intelligence
- Import real squad and market data
- Opponent and standings awareness
- Maximum bid recommendation
- Buy / hold / sell engine
- Last-minute alert center

### Phase 3: Habit and retention
- Morning briefing
- Pre-deadline mode
- Push alerts for lineup changes
- Weekly review and strategy recap
- Saved playbooks by competition

## Non-goals right now

- Competing by article volume alone
- Covering every league before dominating one
- Rebuilding the fantasy game itself
- Shipping social features before the decision engine is sharp

## The bar

If a user opens the app 20 minutes before the deadline, they should leave with:
- a final lineup
- a confidence score
- one market action
- one player to watch
- less doubt than before opening the app
